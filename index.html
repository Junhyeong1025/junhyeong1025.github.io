Postdoctoral Fellow · PRISM-AI Center (InnoCORE) & AiM4 Lab · KAIST

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the PRISM-AI Center and AiM4 Lab, Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), advised by Prof. Seunghwa Ryu. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from KAIST in 2026.

Currently, my research focuses on AI-driven inverse design, AI for end-to-end manufacturing, data-driven optimization, and Large Language Models applied to materials, structures, and manufacturing systems.

Research Interests

To borrow a term from optimization: the scientist in me explores what AI is genuinely good for, and the engineer in me exploits it for design and manufacturing. My guiding question is how do we make design and manufacturing systems something people can simply talk to? That question shapes my work in the following directions.

  • 🎯

    Strategic Use of AI for Design & Manufacturing

    Exploring how AI can be applied effectively and practically to real engineering problems across design and manufacturing.

    Representative: Inverse Design Methods Review (Materials Horizons, 2023)  ·  LLM Perspective (engrXiv, 2026)

  • 🏭

    Intelligent Design & Manufacturing Systems

    Building domain-specialized multi-agent LLM systems that autonomously carry out materials design, structural design, process optimization, and monitoring.

    Representative: IM-Chat (Multi-agent LLM) (J. Manuf. Syst., 2025)

  • 🧩

    Integration of Knowledge- & Physics-Driven AI with Data-Driven AI

    Integrating domain knowledge and governing physics into data-driven models to keep predictions reliable where data is scarce.

    Representative: Knowledge-Guided AI for Inverse Design (Adv. Intell. Discov., 2025)  ·  LLM Material Generation for Double Perovskites (arXiv, 2025)
